The Güemes

You are a drone pilot at a farm in Argentina. Everything is dark until your drone's camera has seen it, and the day goes by while you fly.

Day 1: find the lagoon and land on the island. Day 2: take a picture of the 7 cows and 2 horses, all together.

Windows 10 / 11 (64-bit) Controller or keyboard 36 MB
Download for Windows

Unzip the file, then run TheGuemes.exe from inside the folder. Windows may say it doesn't recognise the app: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.
